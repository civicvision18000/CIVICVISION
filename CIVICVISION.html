Reference Portal Guidelines

1. How does Call to Report work?

The Call to Report button is configured with our toll-free helpline (1800). Clicking it initiates a standard telephone call on supported mobile devices. On desktop computers without phone hardware, please dial 1800 directly from your mobile or landline telephone.

2. Which languages are supported for Voice Reporting?

Voice Reporting fully supports Tamil (ta-IN), English (en-IN), Malayalam (ml-IN), Kannada (kn-IN), and Telugu (te-IN). Your spoken statement is processed using the selected native locale, preserving your original transcript without substitution.

3. How is photographic evidence verified?

Uploaded photos are analyzed for visual clarity, lighting exposure, and physical detail. If an image is too dark or washed out, the portal classifies it as "Insufficient Evidence" and invites you to re-upload. Clear photos simply allow field teams to respond faster.
